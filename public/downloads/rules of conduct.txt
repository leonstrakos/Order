Rules of Conduct of the Heralds
Of the Order of the Lion’s Bell

I	On the Path of the Herald 
ΓΝΩΘΙ ΣΕΑΥΤΟΝ 
Every Herald shall pursue Wisdom, and mastery of the self, for a mind that is undisciplined cannot bear the weight of knowledge. 

A chivalric society is to be founded upon loyalty  honor and dignity among its members, so that their conduct reflects the light of the Order.  Each Herald shall regard another as kin in the pursuit of truth. A Herald shall give knowledge, compassion, and strength where they are needed. Let no Herald turn away from a plea for aid.

II	Against the Slumber of Habit
A Herald shall not become a homo consuetudinis for the world is vast and the human spirit was not made for cages of routine. 

Stagnation is the slow death of soul and waste of existence. A Herald shall walk unfamiliar roads, discover unknown art, immerse themselves in cultures, speak with strangers, and learn the distant tongues as they are doors to new perception, through exploration the soul remembers its beauty.

III	On Body and Health
The Herald must care for their health, strength, and endurance.
The Herald must strive to be Kaloikagathoi.

IV	On Food and Ethical Living
The human being is a biological and conscious system. Every substance taken into body influences perception, clarity, vitality, and awareness. A Herald must treat food as sacred fuel for the body and his consciousness. 
The Herald shall seek nourishment that strengthens life, sharpens the mind, and preserves longevity. And must avoid excess, artificiality, and that which corrupts natural function,  which degrades the body.
Herald shall seek to lengthen his existence with gratitude. As to knowingly degrade one’s body through neglect or excess is to act against the discipline of the Order.

V	On Humanity and Humility
A Herald must remember always he is an animal who learned to dream.
Therefore let humility and gratitude guide the Herald.
The Herald must practice both art and science, for together they form the full expression of humanity.

VI	On Qualia and Experience
Each consciousness experiences existence in a manner unique and unrepeatable. The Order honors the sacred individuality of perception and experience. A Herald must respect the inner world of every being. 
Herald's duty is to live life of discovery and through their own experience and return those into shared memory of the humankind.

VII	On the Entry
Those who seek to enter must show kindness of heart, clarity of mind, and sincerity of purpose. Membership in the Order is a privilege, but not a status, title, or authority.

VIII	The Great Sins of the Order
To live in the shadow of shame, or to impose it upon another, is to deny the dignity of existence.
To surrender one’s life to unconscious repetition is to abandon the gift of awareness.

IX 	On Free Will and Expulsion
The Order commands no soul, for every being walks by the power of their own will. 
Nothing is required of a Herald that they have not freely chosen.
Yet actions that repeatedly violate the spirit of the Order weaken the whole.
Those who repeatedly violate the principles with great sins of of the Order may be expelled, not in punishment, but for the preservation of integrity of the fellowship


X	On the Purpose of the Heralds
Knowledge belongs to all humanity, and the Herald is its messenger.
Therefore The Herald is there to represent the highest potential of the human species, in matter, energy  and consciousness.


IDEAS BASED ON (so far) : Biosemiotics, panpsychism, kaloigatakhia
