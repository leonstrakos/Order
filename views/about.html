Ordo Praeconum Campanae Leonis

The Order of the Heralds of the Lion's Bell was founded as a philosophical and chivalric fellowship devoted to awakening, the pursuit of knowledge, and the proclamation of truth.

✶✶

The Order draws inspiration from mystical philosophy, ancient cosmology, hermetic traditions, gnostic thought, and the symbolic teachings of the mystery schools.

✶✶

Rather than claiming a single religious authority, the Order studies these traditions as symbolic paths toward a deeper understanding of reality and truth.

✶✶

Members of the Order are known as Heralds of the Lion.
